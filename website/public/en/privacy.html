Privacy

This page explains the data used by the Nawam website and its limitations. This policy applies to this website; third-party services have their own policies.

A website without accounts

This website does not offer registration, login, data collection forms, advertising, or analytics. Its code does not intentionally create application cookies or store visitor preferences.

Hosting and downloads

Pages, fonts, and images are served through Firebase Hosting. Application and source packages are downloaded through GitHub Releases; GitHub's privacy policy applies to those requests. Infrastructure providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, request times, user agents, and security and operational logs. See Google's privacy policy.

Clipboard

The “Copy checksum” button only writes the SHA-256 checksum to the clipboard when you press it. This website does not read your clipboard contents.

External links

Documentation, source, and developer links may open services outside the Nawam website. The destination service's privacy policy applies when you visit it.

Desktop application

The website cannot see your drives, ISOs, or USB contents. Nawam processes drives and images locally. Some boot components may require network downloads; automatic application updates are not available yet.

Nawam settings are stored in an application-specific registry location or in nawam.ini in portable mode. Logs contain technical information that may include drive names and file paths. Review logs and remove personal information before sharing them.

Contact the developer

Developer information is available at shanna.id. The website does not ask for passwords, tokens, or access to your drives.
